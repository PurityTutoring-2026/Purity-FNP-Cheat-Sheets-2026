How to use these cheat sheets
  1. Pick a topic from the Cheat sheet menu, or use ‹ Previous sheet and Next sheet ›.
  2. Read the whole sheet once in about 5 minutes. Bold words are the facts most often tested.
  3. Tick Quiz me (hide answers) to blur the answer columns. Say the answer out loud, then tap a blurred box to check it.
  4. Use Show all answers to reveal the sheet again, then move to the next topic.
